# Code Of Conduct

Snap welcomes constructive participation from developers with different experience levels.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent, especially with beginners.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable.
- Discuss safety-sensitive behavior carefully and factually.
- Avoid harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Threats or harassment.
- Doxing or sharing private information.
- Repeated disruptive comments.
- Pressure to run destructive commands without clear warnings.
- Public disclosure of security issues before responsible reporting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocking from the project spaces.
